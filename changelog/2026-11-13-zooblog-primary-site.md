# ZooBlog primary site

The public delivery of the Zoositioweb catalog uses the site recorded at publication.
ZooBeriah Systems is the default for existing and new articles. The selected site
uses its article URL as canonical. The other site keeps a readable copy with
a noindex directive. Editorial access stays with the Zoositioweb Content Hub.

The rollout originally defaulted all shared articles to ZooBeriah Systems.
Limit the implicit ZooBeriah choice to the Blog Builder SEO and welcome articles.
Other shared articles default to Zoositioweb. Explicit published selections and
article-level indexing restrictions continue to apply.
